# Geography and substrate — v9.2.0

Coastline: Natural Earth 1:50m land polygons (public domain), already bundled in world-land-*.js. Local land masks use a 100 km square at 1024 samples, with an urban dry apron. These are schematic, not surveyed shorelines.

Substrate: RESOLVE Ecoregions 2017, Dinerstein et al., An Ecoregion-Based Approach to Protecting Half the Terrestrial Realm, https://doi.org/10.1093/biosci/bix014. License CC-BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Dataset: https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/RESOLVE_ECOREGIONS_2017 . Public distribution: https://services.arcgis.com/P3ePLMYs2RVChkJx/arcgis/rest/services/Resolve_Ecoregions/FeatureServer/0 .

This project extracts biome 13 (deserts and xeric shrublands) and biome 11 (tundra), simplifies polygons by 0.025 degrees, and omits very small fragments and Antarctica. Desert classification describes a regional arid substrate; it does not assert that every desert is dune sand. Urban lawns remain cultivated gardens. Irrigated valleys and non-arid regions remain distinct. This is regional approximation, not parcel-level land-cover data.

Intercity roads share the map graph and local east/north directions. Connections terminate at actual graph destinations; roads beyond the regional view are clipped inside atmospheric fog. No synthetic island loops are added. Mountain roads use contour search and follow the actual triangulated terrain surface.

Biome weights are spatially softened before shading; vegetation density uses the same continuous weights. Far woodland uses procedural instanced silhouettes and canopy patches up to 52 km, overlapping the near geometry from 4.7 to 6.6 km. Neighbor previews use east/north projection from the same coordinates as road endpoints. Scene handoff rebases camera coordinates without rotating the view. These are regional approximations, not surveyed terrain or real building reconstructions.

## v9.28.0 — complete environmental classification
The previous renderer equated all RESOLVE biome 13 with bare sandy desert. It now uses all 14 biomes, 847 source features and 91,484 polygons, downloaded 2026-11-18 with maxAllowableOffset 0.005 degrees. RESOLVE 2017, CC BY 4.0: https://services.arcgis.com/P3ePLMYs2RVChkJx/arcgis/rest/services/Resolve_Ecoregions/FeatureServer/0 .
Climate: Beck et al. (2023), Scientific Data 10,724, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-02549-6 , CC BY 4.0. Historical 1991–2020 classes remain at the original 1/120 degree grid. COG distribution: https://data.naturalcapitalalliance.stanford.edu/dataset/sts-b04939b0df93eb3f4305a065933c66122a0edc6fa425b157b99aa7b4b4446d20 . Two-degree latitude bands are loaded for the current city; at most four decoded bands are retained.
Bare arid ground requires xeric biome AND BWh/BWk climate. Dry forests, xeric scrub, savannas, Mediterranean vegetation and tundra have distinct tints and tree densities. Boundaries are blended. Coastline masks remain 1024-square; environmental textures use 256-square smoothly filtered fields, consistent with kilometre-scale source data.
Coastal gaps use the closest ecoregion within 2.5 km. Climate ocean-cell gaps use the closest pixel within two pixels. Seventeen quarter-degree cells covering nineteen catalogue locations absent from both sources use a derived, coarser NASA POWER MERRA-2 monthly climate estimate: https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/temporal/climatology/ . Requests and periods are preserved in climate-supplement.json. The Maldives moist-forest biome is supported by https://www.cbd.int/pa/doc/dossiers/maldives-abt11-country-dossier2021.pdf . Visitors never call a remote climate API.
All 236,953 catalogue coordinates in 246 country/territory files were checked: 233,567 inside source polygons; 3,060 bounded coastal assignments; 326 regional climate estimates; zero unclassified entries. The latter groups are approximations. All 235,132 available source climate pixels match the original 1-km GeoTIFF. 7,191 locations no longer receive the previous desert appearance. This simulates regional ecology; it is not current satellite land cover or parcel-level soil mapping.


### Neighbor previews and flight clearance (v9.28.0)
Preview roof/body footprints reserve free space outside developed city blocks and regional roads, and cannot overlap other preview buildings. Geographic city centers and directory entries are unchanged. Centers inside the current urban fabric are entered explicitly from their label/menu; automatic handoff inside active blocks is suppressed. Flight collision grids cover complete city bird/balloon orbits plus 120m, rather than indexing entire 100km mountain bounding boxes. Nearby terrain triangles remain collision obstacles. Regression: 441 actual terrain raycasts, 500 equal local queries, 20,000-instance reference checks, crowded 31-city preview fixture with no building/block/road overlaps.


## v9.29.0 — growth-aware urban areas
Regional urban components use complete permanent lot-template bounds for each known city population and shop count, including unlocked civic parcels and milestones above 6,000 homes. Population growth recomputes components; intersecting footprints share one regional representation. The active component never spawns independent miniature cities or city labels over the open city. Other components use one representative preview and label; every original center remains in the nearby-places menu and retains its own houses/points. Real geographic coordinates, roads and building lots are unchanged. This is a visual agglomeration rule for the procedural game, not a change to administrative borders.

District metadata: GeoNames cities500 snapshot already bundled in the project, CC BY 4.0. 10,779 PPLX identifiers are recognized as sections of a populated place: https://www.geonames.org/export/codes.html . São Paulo official district references: https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/licenciamento/w/servicos/312207 . No city-name special cases are used. Menu descriptions are provided in all ten supported languages.

Tests compare predicted bounds against actual allocated lots at 1–200,000 homes. Growth grouping was exercised on real São Paulo-region coordinates at 100, 500, 5,000, 20,000 and 100,000 homes, and on a growing neighboring city whose center remains outside the active city. These are layout/placement tests, not rendering-performance claims at 200,000 homes.
